# Fractional CTO vs Full-Time CTO — Cost Calculator

A small interactive calculator built for [prommer.net](https://prommer.net) as a work-sample test.

**The gap it fills:** the Tech & AI advisory side of prommer.net has lots of guides and a "Schedule Discovery Call" button, but nothing interactive in between. This calculator lets a company visitor compare what a full-time CTO hire would cost against a fractional engagement, then leads to that same call to action.

**Built on the site's own published numbers:** a full-time CTO at "$400K+ annual cost", and the three engagement models (10–15 hrs/week Strategic Advisory, 15–20 hrs/week Active Leadership, Project-Based). Source: [prommer.net/en/tech/services/fractional-cto](https://prommer.net/en/tech/services/fractional-cto/).
