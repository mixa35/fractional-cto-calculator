## Why this project exists

This is a **job application test**. I applied for a role and was given a work-sample task. The project exists to pass that test. It is not a long-term product, and it is not a client engagement.

The task, as given to me:

> **Ship one small real thing for prommer.net, AI-driven.**
> Pick something tiny enough to finish in this window. A landing page, a static calculator, a Notion template, a small script — anything you can publish and link to.

What the reviewers are likely judging:
- **Judgment:** did I pick something that actually matters to the site?
- **Speed:** did I finish inside the time window?
- **AI-native workflow:** did I use AI to build it, and can I show that?
- **Shipping:** is there a real, working, public link at the end?

The finished thing matters, but the reasoning behind it and a clean handoff matter just as much.

## Who the site belongs to

**prommer.net** is the personal site of **Thomas Prommer**, a technology executive (CTO / CIO, fractional leadership, applied AI) who is also a hybrid endurance athlete (Ironman, marathon, HYROX). Thomas Prommer also runs **We The Flywheel**, an AI-native, agentic-led digital studio. That's likely the company behind this application, which is why the folder has that name.

The site has two halves:
- **Tech & AI advisory:** services (Fractional CTO, Fractional CIO, AI Leadership, Chief Technology & AI Officer) plus many long-form guides. The only call to action is **"Schedule Discovery Call"**. **This side has no interactive tools.**
- **Training:** 15+ interactive calculators (HYROX pace, training zones, VDOT, fueling, FTP, and more).

The site is minimal and data-heavy, with a navy/neutral palette. It openly publishes its own AI usage (AI-paired commits, token usage by model).

## What we are building

**A Fractional CTO vs Full-Time CTO cost calculator for prommer.net.**

A company visitor answers a few simple questions about their situation and gets a clear comparison: what a full-time CTO hire would cost versus a fractional engagement with Thomas Prommer. It ends on the site's existing "Schedule Discovery Call" call to action.

Why this idea was chosen over the alternatives:
- The advisory side is where the business makes money, and it has nothing interactive between reading a guide and booking a call. This tool fills that gap.
- It is built on Thomas Prommer's **own published numbers**, so it doesn't depend on outside data that could be wrong:
  - Full-time CTO: **"$400K+ annual cost"**
  - Engagement models: **10–15 hrs/week Strategic Advisory**, **15–20 hrs/week Active Leadership**, **Project-Based** specific initiatives
  - Source: https://prommer.net/en/tech/services/fractional-cto/
- The training side already proves the site can build calculators. This shows I noticed what's missing, not just copied what's there.

Ideas considered and parked: AI coding tool ROI calculator (runner-up), AI readiness scorecard, marathon pace / race time predictor, Boston qualifier tracker (rejected because its qualifying times change yearly and a wrong number would look bad).
