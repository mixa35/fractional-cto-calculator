How these numbers are calculated
  • Full-time CTO: the yearly all-in cost you set. It starts at $400K, the figure prommer.net gives for a full-time hire.
  • Full-time equivalent rate: full-time cost ÷ 2,080 hours (40 hours × 52 weeks). The hourly rate slider starts here, so every starting number comes from that single $400K figure.
  • Fractional cost: hours per week × hourly rate × 52 weeks. The hours come from prommer.net's published engagement models: 10–15 hrs a week (Strategic Advisory) and 15–20 hrs a week (Active Leadership). The solid bar shows the low end of each range; the striped part extends to the high end.
  • Project-Based: priced per initiative, so no yearly figure is shown.
  • Best fit: a simple rule of thumb. One specific initiative points to Project-Based. Needing someone to build and lead the team, or having more than 15 engineers, points to Active Leadership. Everything else points to Strategic Advisory.
  • This is an estimate for comparison, not a quote.
